About Us

A Family Craft Since 1879

We have been cutting and lettering stone for the same reason for nearly 150 years. A memorial is a personal thing and it stands for generations, so we listen first, we take the time it takes, and we never push anybody towards a decision they are not ready to make.

Every appointment is completely free of charge and there is no obligation at the end of it. We will meet you here at the workshop, at the churchyard or cemetery, or at home, whichever is easiest.
